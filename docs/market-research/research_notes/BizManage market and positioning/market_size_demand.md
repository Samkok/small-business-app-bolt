# Market size and demand for mobile POS + inventory apps for micro and small retailers (SEA focus, global English secondary)

Research date: 15 November 2026. Older data is marked with its year. "Est." marks my own arithmetic or estimates, not a source figure. Many figures come from search-engine summaries of pages; where I fetched or extracted the primary document myself, that is noted ("primary, extracted").

## 1. How many micro/small retail businesses exist, and how informal are they?

### Takeaway
SEA-6 plus Cambodia hold roughly 60+ million MSMEs (Indonesia alone ~56 million), about 97% of all firms, and the great majority are micro, sole-proprietor, trade/service businesses outside capital cities. Cambodia has ~754,000 establishments (2022), about two-thirds in wholesale/retail trade and 96.5% sole proprietorships. Worldwide there are ~420-510 million MSMEs, about three quarters informal.

### Cited Findings
**Cambodia**
- Economic Census of Cambodia 2022: 753,670 establishments employing 2.98 million people, up from 505,134 establishments and 1.67 million workers in the 2011 census — [NIS final report EC2022](https://nis.gov.kh/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/National-Report-on-Final-Census-Results-Economic-Census-of-Cambodia-2022.pdf); [Open Development Cambodia](https://opendevelopmentcambodia.net/news/cambodia-records-753670-economic-entities-census/); [Cambodia Investment Review, Jan 2024](https://cambodiainvestmentreview.com/2024/01/09/cambodias-economic-census-surveys-750k-entities-with-combined-annual-sales-of-50-7b/)
- EC2022: the section "Wholesale and retail trade; repair of motor vehicles and motorcycles" is the largest, at 65.6% of all establishments; sole proprietorships are 96.5% of establishments — same sources as above (per search summaries of the NIS report)
- Est.: 65.6% x 753,670 ≈ 494,000 trade establishments in Cambodia (2022). My calculation; the exact count is in NIS Table 2.2.1.
- EC2011 (older): 97.7% of businesses were micro (<10 workers) and 79% had fewer than three workers — [Open Development Cambodia, EC2011 summary](https://opendevelopmentcambodia.net/news/national-census-identifies-half-million-businesses/)
- ADB (2014 Inter-Censal Economic Survey, the latest MSME-definition data Cambodia has published): 513,759 establishments, 99.8% (512,870) MSMEs; around 60% in wholesale and retail trade; 81.2% located outside Phnom Penh. ADB notes there is no updated MSME data since 2014 under the MSME definition — [ADB Asia SME Monitor 2025, Cambodia chapter (primary, extracted)](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf)
- A CDRI report (Nov 2025) draws on the EC2022 data for 5,694 wholesale/retail MSMEs — [CDRI, Assessing MSME](https://cdri.org.kh/storage/pdf/Res%20report_Assessing%20MSME_1764141762.pdf) (I could not open it; the file is over 10 MB)

**Other SEA markets (ADB Asia SME Monitor 2025, primary, extracted)**
- Indonesia: number of MSMEs contracted 0.5% to 56.2 million in 2024 (labor force survey basis) — [ADB ASM 2025](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf). Older figures: 65.46 million (2019), 64 million (2020, Ministry of Cooperatives and SMEs) — [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1317131/asean-number-of-micro-small-medium-sized-businesses-by-country); [Indonesia Business Post](https://indonesiabusinesspost.com/194/asean/indonesia-is-targeting-30-million-msmes-digitalization-in-asean)
- Malaysia: MSMEs fell 1.4% to 1.1 million (2024) — [ADB ASM 2025](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf)
- Philippines: MSMEs fell 0.4% to 1.2 million (2024), workforce 6.3 million. These are registered establishments; the informal sector is far larger — [ADB ASM 2025](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf)
- Thailand: MSMEs grew 0.9% to 3.3 million firms (2024), 68.8% of workers — [ADB ASM 2025](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf)
- Viet Nam: 757,788 MSME *enterprises* in 2023 (97.3% of enterprises); wholesale and retail trade is 39.0% of them; MSMEs employ 37.1% of the workforce — [ADB ASM 2025](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf). These exclude household businesses.
- Viet Nam household businesses (unincorporated, the micro-retail core): about five million household businesses moving to the self-declared tax system from 2026 — [Thoi bao Tai chinh Viet Nam, Dec 2025](https://thoibaotaichinhvietnam.vn/five-million-household-businesses-to-adopt-self-declared-tax-system-188612.html); [Vietstock](https://en.vietstock.vn/2025/12/five-million-household-businesses-to-adopt-self-declared-tax-system-37-626118.htm)
- Viet Nam retail outlets: approximately 1.9 million retail shops (2020, cited by a KiotViet executive) — [e27, 2020](https://e27.co/our-main-barrier-to-growth-is-status-quo-in-retail-sector-kiotviets-deputy-gm-tri-cao-20200605/)

**Regional structure**
- On average MSMEs are 97% of all enterprises and 69% of the workforce in Southeast Asia — [ADB SEADS](https://seads.adb.org/news/7-ways-bolster-southeast-asias-fragile-msmes)
- Developing Asia and the Pacific (2024 data): MSMEs are 99.8% of enterprises and 67.6% of the workforce; an average of 72% of MSMEs work in traditional services such as trade, accommodation and food, and 73% operate outside the capital city — [ADB ASM 2025 (primary, extracted)](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf)

**Global**
- 420-510 million MSMEs worldwide, of which only about 9% are formal; 365-445 million are in emerging markets (undated World Bank-derived figures in a UNCITRAL presentation) — [UNCITRAL / Uttamchandani](https://uncitral.un.org/sites/uncitral.un.org/files/media-documents/uncitral/en/c_msme_1_uttamchandani.pdf)
- 280-340 million MSMEs are informal firms, around 74% of all MSMEs worldwide — [UNESCAP MSME typologies](https://msmepolicy.unescap.org/typologies-msmes)
- MSME finance gap of about US$5.7 trillion across 119 emerging and developing economies — [IFC/World Bank MSME Finance Gap Report, Mar 2025](https://www.smefinanceforum.org/news/11541348)

### Inferences
- The addressable base of shops in BizManage's home market is about 0.5 million trade establishments in Cambodia (est.), almost all sole proprietors and mostly outside Phnom Penh. Cambodia is a small market: even 1% paid penetration is ~5,000 shops, or about $600k ARR at $120/yr (est.).
- Viet Nam (~5 million household businesses, ~1.9 million retail shops) and Indonesia (~56 million MSMEs) are 10-100x larger by count, but both already have strong local incumbents (see section 5).
- Official MSME counts for the Philippines (1.2M) and Malaysia (1.1M) cover registered firms only; the informal sari-sari / micro-retail base is much larger and not counted here.
- Phone-only and family-run status is not measured directly anywhere I found. The high sole-proprietor share (96.5% in Cambodia) and the micro share (<3 workers for 79% of Cambodian firms in 2011) are the best proxies.

### Gaps
- No exact EC2022 count of Cambodian retail-only establishments (excluding wholesale and vehicle repair), or of the unregistered share. Both should be in NIS Table 2.2.1 and the EC2022 wholesale/retail report.
- No reliable informal-retail counts for the Philippines, Indonesia or Thailand. ADB counts are labor-survey or registration based.
- No direct measure of "phone-only" shops (no PC, no POS terminal) for any SEA market.
- Global count of small retailers specifically (vs all MSMEs) not found.

## 2. Digital adoption: smartphones, paper/Excel, POS or bookkeeping apps

### Takeaway
Connectivity is no longer the barrier: SEA mobile internet penetration reached 76% at end-2025 and almost all mobile internet users are on smartphones. What is missing is reliable data on back-office use. I found no credible survey giving the share of SEA micro retailers using paper notebooks vs apps; research (ERIA/RIETI) says adoption is broad but shallow.

### Cited Findings
- Southeast Asia mobile internet penetration rose almost 20 percentage points in five years to 76% at end-2025, led by Malaysia, Thailand and Viet Nam — [GSMA press release](https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/press-release/1-2-billion-people-across-developing-asia-pacific-remain-offline-despite-near-universal-mobile-coverage/)
- In Developing Asia Pacific, 93% of mobile internet users connect with a 4G or 5G smartphone — [GSMA, State of Mobile Internet Connectivity 2025](https://www.gsma.com/somic/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/The-State-of-Mobile-Internet-Connectivity-2025-Overview-Report.pdf)
- Cambodia mobile connections dipped 2.4% between start-2024 and start-2025 (GSMA Intelligence); Cambodia has not launched 5G — [Open Development Cambodia](https://opendevelopmentcambodia.net/news/mobile-phone-connections-dip-2-4-percent-in-kingdom/); [GSMA](https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/press-release/1-2-billion-people-across-developing-asia-pacific-remain-offline-despite-near-universal-mobile-coverage/)
- Cambodia mobile banking users passed 10 million (2026) — [Cambodia Investment Review, 2026](https://cambodiainvestmentreview.com/2026/06/08/exclusive-cambodias-digital-economy-reaches-new-milestone-as-mobile-banking-users-surpass-10-million-and-khqr-payments-exceed-105-billion-annually/)
- Indonesia, the Philippines and Cambodia are named as markets where large non-urban populations are still held back by handset affordability, skills or relevance — [GSMA, handset affordability 2025](https://www.gsma.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Breaking-barriers-handset-affordability.pdf)
- A 2026 RIETI/ERIA firm-level study of Indonesia, Malaysia and Viet Nam (23 digital tools) separates adoption breadth from depth. It concludes that policy should build digital use capability, not just expand adoption — [RIETI DP 26-E-058](https://www.rieti.go.jp/en/publications/summary/26070008.html)
- ERIA finds a significant digital divide among ASEAN MSMEs — [ERIA, The Digital Divide Amongst MSMEs in ASEAN](https://www.eria.org/research/the-digital-divide-amongst-msmes-in-asean)
- Over 80% of Malaysian MSMEs "embrace digitalisation" (Visa whitepaper, Nov 2025); this is a broad definition, not POS or bookkeeping use — [Visa Malaysia](https://www.visa.com.my/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases/over-80-percent-of-malaysian-msmes-embrace-digitalisation-visa-whitepaper-identifies-four-pathways-to-accelerate-expansion.html)
- Proxy from South Asia: in a 2025 Visa study of 250 small retailers in Pakistan, 40% accepted cash only — [Visa Pakistan, May 2025](https://pk.visamiddleeast.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases/prl-19052025.html)
- Adoption proxies from app vendors (company claims): Loyverse 350,000+ active merchants in 170+ countries — [Loyverse](https://loyverse.com); KiotViet 300,000 stores (undated) and 100,000 stores with a claimed 50% share in 2020 — [KiotViet about page](https://about.kiotviet.vn/?p=260); [e27, 2020](https://e27.co/our-main-barrier-to-growth-is-status-quo-in-retail-sector-kiotviets-deputy-gm-tri-cao-20200605/); BukuWarung 6.5 million merchants (2021) — [Jakarta Post, 2021](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2021/06/11/sme-bookkeeping-start-up-bukuwarung-bags-60m-series-a-funding.html); Vyapar "1.5 crore+ active businesses" — [Vyapar](https://vyaparapp.in/success-stories)

### Inferences
- Est.: KiotViet's claimed 300,000 stores against ~1.9 million Vietnamese retail shops suggests about 15% POS software penetration in Viet Nam's most mature micro-retail software market. The rest of the region is probably lower. Both numbers are company or older figures.
- A smartphone-only app fits the hardware reality (smartphones near universal among internet users; PCs and terminals rare in micro shops). The harder barrier is capability and habit, which supports a simple onboarding and a free tier.

### Gaps
- No credible SEA survey found on the share of micro retailers using paper notebooks, Excel, or any POS/bookkeeping app. The DAI/Ipsos MSME country briefs ([Cambodia](https://www.dai.com/uploads/final-msme-reports/cambodia-country-brief.pdf)) and the ERIA full report may contain bookkeeping questions, but I did not open them.
- No smartphone-ownership rate specifically for SME owners in any SEA country.
- No World Bank Enterprise Survey data pulled for bookkeeping or computer use in Cambodia (the latest Cambodia WBES is 2016 and covers formal firms only).

## 3. Mobile/cloud POS and inventory software market size and growth

### Takeaway
Analyst reports put global POS software at roughly $12-16 billion in 2025, growing 11-12% a year, with SMEs about 57-61% of spend and Asia-Pacific the fastest-growing region (11.6% CAGR for POS software, ~20% for cloud POS in Mordor's reports). These numbers vary 3x between publishers and say nothing reliable about phone-only micro retail, which is mostly free or under $20/month and barely shows in revenue-based sizing.

### Cited Findings
- Mordor Intelligence: POS software $16.05 billion (2025) to $32.33 billion (2031), 12.38% CAGR 2026-2031; SMEs hold 60.55% of 2025 share and grow faster (12.41% CAGR); Asia-Pacific is the top growth region at 11.58% CAGR — [Mordor POS software](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/pos-software-market)
- Mordor Intelligence, cloud POS: SMEs 57.30% of 2025 revenue; North America 34.70% of revenue; Asia-Pacific fastest at 19.92% CAGR to 2031 — [Mordor cloud POS](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/cloud-point-of-sale-market)
- Another publisher: global POS software $11.90 billion (2025) to $23.26 billion (2031), 11.82% CAGR — [GlobeNewswire / Research and Markets, Apr 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/16/3275221/0/en/POS-Software-Markets-and-Competition-Analysis-2026-2034-Retail-Sector-Dominates-Embracing-Digital-Transformation.html)
- Mobile POS reports mix hardware (mPOS terminals) and software and disagree on size and even on the leading region — e.g. [Fortune Business Insights mPOS](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/mobile-point-of-sale-pos-Industry-102994); [GMI mPOS terminals](https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/mobile-point-of-sale-mpos-terminals-market%20); [Straits Research](https://straitsresearch.com/report/mobile-point-of-sale-system-market)
- Viet Nam POS SaaS: over 100 providers competing; the report's headline market value (USD 1.2 billion) is not clearly sourced — [Ken Research via MarketResearch.com](https://www.marketresearch.com/Ken-Research-v3771/Vietnam-Smart-Retail-POS-SaaS-44260631)

### Inferences
- Methodology warning for the report writer: these figures are top-down vendor-revenue models with no published sampling. They are dominated by North American and enterprise spend (Square, Toast, Lightspeed, Oracle). Use them only as "the category is ~$12-16B and growing ~12%", not to size SEA micro retail.
- A bottom-up sizing is more useful for BizManage. Est.: Cambodia ~0.5M trade establishments x 5% that pay x $120/yr ≈ $3M/yr ceiling at today's price. Viet Nam ~1.9M retail shops x 10% x $120 ≈ $23M/yr; Indonesia and the Philippines are larger by count but lower in price tolerance. These are illustrative assumptions, not sourced figures.

### Gaps
- No analyst figure for SEA-only or phone-only POS/inventory software.
- No Asia-Pacific POS software dollar figure for 2025 that I could verify (paywalled).

## 4. Payment context: QR, cash, cash on delivery, social commerce

### Takeaway
Every ASEAN market now has a national interoperable QR scheme and over 60% of SEA payments are digital, but cash and COD still matter, especially for social-commerce orders. In Cambodia, KHQR/Bakong has scaled fast (over 1 billion transactions a year, ~10 million mobile banking users), and selling runs heavily through Facebook, TikTok and Telegram with COD. That mix of cash, transfer, COD and chat orders is exactly where a free bank merchant app stops short.

### Cited Findings
- e-Conomy SEA 2025 (10th edition, Nov 2025): over 60% of payments in the region are digital and three in five people shop online; all ASEAN markets now operate national unified QR systems; regional digital GMV is projected to pass $300 billion; coverage expanded to all ten ASEAN markets including Cambodia (2025 figures are annualised H1 2025) — [Bain press release](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/sea/e-conomy-sea-2025/); [Temasek](https://www.temasek.com.sg/en/news-and-resources/news-room/news/2025/e-conomy-sea-2025-report-aseans-digital-economy-poised-to-surpass-300-billion); [report PDF](https://www.temasek.com.sg/content/dam/temasek-corporate/news-and-views/resources/reports/e-Conomy-sea-2025-report.pdf)
- Cambodia, Bakong 2025: about 1.33 billion transactions across riel and dollar accounts; reported values conflict between sources — [Vietstock / Bakong coverage](https://en.vietstock.vn/2025/03/bakong-volume-now-more-than-300-percent-of-cambodias-gdp-114-606221.htm); [NBC press release, Nov 2025](https://www.fintechfestival.sg/hubfs/SFF%20-%202025/SFF%202025%20-%20Website/Media,%20Join%20Us/NBC_PR_13Nov2025_LG%20input%20(3)%20(1).pdf)

### Inferences
- QR makes "payment method" a real reconciliation problem: a shop takes cash, KHQR from several banks, COD via delivery riders, and chat orders. Tracking cash vs transfer vs COD per sale, plus delivery fees, matches this.
- Dual-currency (riel/USD) handling is specific to Cambodia (Bakong runs both currencies) and is a real differentiator against regional tools that assume one currency.
- An order link/QR menu that drops orders in as carts fits social selling, where orders arrive by chat and are fulfilled with COD.

### Gaps
- No primary national cash share of retail payments for Cambodia or the region.
- PromptPay (Thailand), QR Ph (Philippines), DuitNow (Malaysia) and VietQR merchant counts were not found in this round.
- No reliable Cambodia COD share from a primary survey; official KHQR merchant count for 2025 unconfirmed.

## 5. Willingness to pay: what micro merchants actually pay

### Takeaway
The market is anchored on "free core plus cheap add-ons". Loyverse's POS is free with $5-25/month add-ons; KiotViet, the paid leader in Viet Nam, charges about VND 270-490k (≈$10-19) per branch per month; bank merchant apps are free. Ledger apps that grew on free (Khatabook, OkCredit, BukuWarung) struggled to convert users and moved to lending and payments. BizManage's $9.99/month for the whole team sits at the low end of paid tools but is still ~4% of Cambodia's GDP per capita a year.

### Cited Findings
- Loyverse pricing (fetched): POS, 31-day analytics, essential inventory with low-stock alerts, loyalty, kitchen/customer displays and multi-store management are free. Paid add-ons: unlimited sales history $5/store/month ($50/yr); employee management $5/employee/month ($50/yr); advanced inventory $25/store/month ($250/yr); 14-day trials — [Loyverse pricing](https://loyverse.com/pricing)
- KiotViet: Support VND 270,000, Professional VND 330,000, Premium VND 490,000 per branch per month (aggregator, checked Oct 2026); 10-day trial — [Softwares.com](https://www.softwares.com/software/kiotviet/pricing). Earlier: from VND 180,000 (US$7.72) a month for SMEs — [KrASIA](https://www.kr-asia.com/this-company-has-quietly-staked-a-corner-in-vietnams-mobile-pos-market-startup-stories). June 2025 promotion advertised use for as little as VND 6,000 a day — [Viet Nam News Bizhub](https://bizhub.vietnamnews.vn/kiotviet-launches-programme-to-ease-e-invoice-switch-for-small-businesses-post377799.html)
- Est.: at ~26,000 VND/USD (my assumption), KiotViet is about $10.4-18.8 per branch per month.
- KiotViet offered free accounting software to household businesses and co-funded cash-register costs during the 2025-26 tax shift — [VCCI](https://www.vcci.com.vn/news/kiotviet-mien-phi-phan-mem-ke-toan-cho-ho-kinh-doanh); [VCCI](https://www.vcci.com.vn/tin-tuc/kiotviet-dong-hanh-cung-ho-kinh-doanh-giam-chi-phi-su-dung-may-tinh-tien)
- ABA Merchant app (Cambodia) is free, with real-time sales, 30-day sales reports, e-receipts, multiple outlets and cashier assignment; no inventory feature found — [ABA Bank](https://ababank.com/aba-merchant-app/)
- Vyapar (India): freemium, main revenue when users move to paid desktop (2020) — [Livemint via IndiaMART](https://corporate.indiamart.com/2020/07/27/an-app-to-turn-the-khata-digital-for-small-businesses-livemint); FY22 revenue about Rs 20 crore with losses up 4.3x to Rs 26.61 crore — [IndiaMART / Entrackr](https://corporate.indiamart.com/2022/09/14/vyapars-revenue-touches-rs-20-cr-in-fy22-losses-shoot-up-over-4x/). Unverified: about 6 lakh mobile and 3 lakh desktop paid users out of ~9 lakh MAU — [GrowthX student project](https://growthx.club/proof-of-work/fintech/vyapar-apps/acquisition-project---vyapar-apps|66041c4327a88450aaf4bc93) (weak source)
- Khatabook/OkCredit grew huge user bases on a free ledger hook and then struggled to convert them; Khatabook's pricing is now "price on request" (opinion piece) — [vmobify](https://vmobify.com/blog/b2b-sme-app-marketing-strategy)
- BukuWarung raised a US$60 million Series A in 2021 (US$80 million total) and claimed 6.5 million merchants; it planned to add lending, savings and insurance — [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2021/06/11/sme-bookkeeping-start-up-bukuwarung-bags-60m-series-a-funding.html); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2052983)
- Cambodia 2025 GDP per capita US$2,818; population 17.8 million — [IMF Article IV, Sep 2026](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/09/28/pr26307-cambodia-imf-executive-board-concludes-2026-article-iv-consultation)

### Inferences
- Est.: $99.99/yr is ≈3.5% of Cambodian GDP per capita and $119.88/yr (monthly) ≈4.3%. For a shop owner the comparison is shop margin, not per-capita income. A minimart turning over a few hundred dollars a day can afford it if the app shows money saved (stock losses, real profit). A street-side micro stall probably cannot.
- BizManage's "whole team" pricing undercuts Loyverse for teams: Loyverse with 3 employees plus advanced inventory costs $25 + $15 = $40/store/month vs BizManage $9.99. That is a strong comparison for English-speaking secondary markets.
- The "first 50 sales free" gate is much tighter than competitors' free tiers (Loyverse free forever, ABA free, KiotViet 10-14 day trial). Evidence from Khatabook/BukuWarung says free ledgers get adoption but not revenue, so a paid gate is defensible, but 50 sales may end before the owner sees the profit and stock value. *(Corrected 15 Nov 2026: the free cap is 50 sales, not 10. This judgement was made at 10 and is weaker at 50.)*

### Gaps
- No published free-to-paid conversion rate for any SEA micro-merchant app.
- No Cambodian survey on willingness to pay for business software.
- BukuWarung's current (2024-26) model and merchant count not found.
- StoreHub (Malaysia), Qashier, Moka/GoBiz (Indonesia), Sapo pricing not collected.

## 6. Which verticals have the strongest need?

### Takeaway
I found no survey that ranks verticals by need. Structural data points to trade: ~60-66% of Cambodian establishments and 39% of Vietnamese MSME enterprises are in wholesale/retail. Vendor positioning (KiotViet: small shops and F&B) suggests minimarts/grocery, fashion, cosmetics, phone accessories and online sellers, all SKU-heavy with stock bought in bulk.

### Cited Findings
- Wholesale/retail is 65.6% of Cambodian establishments (2022) — [NIS EC2022](https://nis.gov.kh/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/National-Report-on-Final-Census-Results-Economic-Census-of-Cambodia-2022.pdf); ~60% of Cambodian MSMEs (2014) — [ADB ASM 2025](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf)
- Viet Nam MSME enterprises: wholesale and retail 39.0%, other services including accommodation and food 22.4% (2023) — [ADB ASM 2025](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf)
- 72% of developing-Asia MSMEs are in traditional services such as trade, accommodation and food — [ADB ASM 2025](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf)
- KiotViet is described as dominant among Vietnamese small shops and F&B outlets (directory characterisation) — [Bitscale directory](https://bitscale.ai/directory/phan-mem-quan-ly-ban-hang-kiotviet)
- Social commerce sellers (fashion, beauty, consumables) dominate Cambodian online selling via Facebook/Telegram/TikTok — [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2025/02/22/booming-platform-economy-offers-convenience-to-consumers-in-cambodia); PROFITENCE notes trust issues for consumable and wearable goods drive COD — [PROFITENCE 2022](https://ezstatic1.ezweb.online/ezweb_1540/html/profitence-ecomm2022report-copy.html)

### Inferences
- Strongest fit for BizManage's feature set (multi-unit carton/piece, landed cost, stock count write-offs, barcode): minimarts and grocery, small wholesalers/distributors, cosmetics and phone-accessory shops (high SKU counts, imported stock, theft/shrinkage risk).
- Online sellers fit the order-link, COD and delivery-fee features.
- Cafes/restaurants are a weaker fit (they need tables, modifiers, kitchen tickets, where Loyverse is strong). Pharmacies need batch/expiry tracking, which is not in the listed features.

### Gaps
- No vertical-level survey on bookkeeping pain or software adoption in SEA.
- No count of Cambodian minimarts, phone shops or cosmetics shops (EC2022 ISIC 4-digit tables would have this).

## 7. Tailwinds and headwinds: tax digitisation and bank-led free apps

### Takeaway
Viet Nam is the clearest tailwind: presumptive (lump-sum) tax for ~5 million household businesses ended in 2026, moving them to self-declaration with revenue-based e-invoice and cash-register duties, which pushes them to record sales. Cambodia's CamInvoice e-invoicing (launched May 2025) is mandatory for government suppliers and is phasing toward large taxpayers; I found no 2026 obligation for small taxpayers. The main headwind is free bank merchant apps and free POS (ABA, Loyverse) that cover sales reporting at zero cost.

### Cited Findings
**Viet Nam**
- From 2026 the tax authority ends presumptive tax collection and moves household businesses to self-declaration and self-payment — [Vietnam Law Magazine](https://vietnamlawmagazine.vn/presumptive-tax-collection-to-be-abolished-from-next-year-75537.html); [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/presumptive-tax-collection-to-be-abolished-from-next-year-892-104817-article.html)
- About five million household businesses affected — [Thoi bao Tai chinh](https://thoibaotaichinhvietnam.vn/five-million-household-businesses-to-adopt-self-declared-tax-system-188612.html)
- Under Decree 68/2026: households with annual revenue of VND 500 million or less are exempt; those at VND 1 billion or more must use e-invoices (from cash registers connected to the tax authority); VND 500 million-1 billion may register voluntarily. The Tax Department clarified the under-500M group is not required to stop using invoices — [Vietnam.vn](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/cuc-thue-doanh-thu-duoi-500-trieu-dong-van-duoc-dung-hoa-don-dien-tu); [Viet An Law](https://vietanlaw.com/new-2026-rules-for-household-enterprises-tax-in-vietnam/)
- Conflicting later report: Decree 141/2026/ND-CP raised the exemption threshold from VND 500 million to VND 1 billion a year; households above VND 1 billion, or that request e-invoices, use cash-register e-invoices — [JPA Vietnam Tax Newsletter, May 2026](https://jpa.vn/vietnam-tax-newsletter-may-2026/) (single source; verify against decree text)
- Debate over a ~$120k (VND ~3 billion) e-invoicing threshold — [VietNamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/debate-heats-up-over-120k-threshold-for-e-invoicing-2476549.html)
- Incumbents moved fast: KiotViet ran an e-invoice switch programme (June 2025) and free accounting software for household businesses — [Bizhub](https://bizhub.vietnamnews.vn/kiotviet-launches-programme-to-ease-e-invoice-switch-for-small-businesses-post377799.html); [VCCI](https://www.vcci.com.vn/news/kiotviet-mien-phi-phan-mem-ke-toan-cho-ho-kinh-doanh). MISA eShop publishes household-business price lists — [MISA eShop](https://www.misaeshop.vn/?p=24283)

**Cambodia**
- CamInvoice launched 12 May 2025; mandatory B2G for central government budget units and their suppliers; GDT runs compliance, the General Department of Digital Economy runs the platform — [EDICOM](https://edicomgroup.com/electronic-invoicing/cambodia); [RTC Suite / VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/05/21/cambodias-new-era-of-e-invoicing-inside-the-caminvoice-mandate/)
- Private sector: one source says phased mandatory B2B from 2026, another says no general B2B/B2C mandate as of early 2026, with large taxpayers likely first; broad B2B by 2027 expected (all secondary) — [Lenorasoft blog, Feb 2026](https://lenorasoft.blogspot.com/2026/02/cambodia-e-invoicing-explained.html); [VATupdate briefing, Jan 2026](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/01/04/briefing-document-podcast-e-invoicing-and-e-reporting-in-cambodia); [Seres](https://blog.groupseres.com/en/e-invoicing-cambodia)
- Records retention: 10 years, or 3 years for small taxpayers — [EDICOM](https://edicomgroup.com/electronic-invoicing/cambodia)
- ADB: Cambodia has no comprehensive MSME law; MSMEs struggle with formal finance due to lack of collateral and financial history — [ADB ASM 2025](https://www.adb.org/sites/default/files/publication/1096981/asia-sme-monitor-2025.pdf)

**Bank-led and free competition**
- ABA Merchant app: free mPOS with real-time sales, 30-day reports, multi-outlet and cashier support — [ABA Bank](https://ababank.com/aba-merchant-app/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/la/app/aba-merchant/id1559488956)
- ACLEDA ToanChet Pay business portal: merchant registration and transaction reports; no inventory — [ACLEDA Securities](https://acledasecurities.com.kh/kh/eng/ps_toanchetpay)
- Banks bundle POS software: Techcombank offered incentives for linking a new account with KiotViet — [Techcombank](https://techcombank.com/en/information/updates/attractive-offers-when-opening-a-new-techcombank-account-and-linking-kiot-viet-sales-software)
- Digital financial services took 45-50% of SEA digital-economy funding in 2025, up from ~30% — [Fintech News SG on e-Conomy SEA 2025](https://fintechnews.sg/121763/ai/economy-sea-2025-key-insights/)

### Inferences
- Viet Nam's reform creates a one-off wave of household businesses that must track revenue, but local incumbents (KiotViet, MISA, Sapo) are already giving tax-compliant tools away. Entering Viet Nam needs cash-register e-invoice integration, which BizManage does not have.
- In Cambodia, tax is not yet a push factor for micro shops. The pitch has to be profit and stock visibility, not compliance. When CamInvoice reaches smaller taxpayers (2027+ per secondary sources), an export or integration path would become a tailwind.
- Bank apps cover "how much came in today" for free. BizManage's case against them is what they lack: inventory, cost of goods, real profit, multi-unit stock, cash and COD in one ledger, and riel/USD.
- Bank funding is flowing to embedded finance. Bookkeeping data has lending value (the BukuWarung path), which could become a partnership angle with Cambodian banks or MFIs.

### Gaps
- Final, official text of Viet Nam Decree 68/2026 and 141/2026 thresholds not verified.
- No official GDT notice on CamInvoice timing for small or medium taxpayers; Cambodia's VAT threshold rules for small taxpayers not covered here.
- Thailand, Philippines (EIS), Indonesia and Malaysia (MyInvois phased e-invoicing) small-business e-invoicing timelines not researched in this round.
- Number of merchants actively using the ABA Merchant app not found.
