## 3. New metadata, ready to paste

Apple indexes title + subtitle + keyword field, and each word only once, so no word repeats across the three.

**Title (26/30)**
```
BizManage: POS & Inventory
```
`Inventory` is the strongest word in both regions; `POS` is the strongest in SEA. The brand stays first so people who saw a TikTok can find it.

**Subtitle (29/30)**
```
Sales, stock & profit tracker
```
Adds `sales`, `stock`, `profit` and `tracker`. Together with the title this forms the combos people type: `inventory tracker`, `sales tracker`, `profit tracker`, `stock pos`, `pos sales`.

**Keyword field, English (U.S.) (100/100 bytes, commas, no spaces)**
```
point,sale,shop,store,retail,small,business,barcode,scanner,expense,cashier,register,bookkeeping,crm
```
Apple matches `point of sale` from `point,sale`; connecting words are not needed. Nothing here repeats a title or subtitle word.

**Second keyword set via an extra localization (95/100 bytes)**
Apple indexes more than one localization per storefront (the US storefront also indexes Spanish (Mexico), for example). Add a second English localization with the same title and subtitle and this keyword field, which doubles the indexed words at no cost:
```
cash,till,grocery,minimart,wholesale,pharmacy,cafe,stocktake,orders,menu,qr,telegram,riel,khmer
```
Which extra localizations the Cambodia storefront indexes must be checked in Apple's "App Store localizations" table before choosing the language; English (U.K.) and Spanish (Mexico) are the usual choices.

## 5. Ratings: the real blocker

Zero ratings after ten months means almost every visitor leaves, whatever the keywords say. The app has no review prompt (`expo-store-review` is not in `package.json`).

1. Add `expo-store-review` and call `requestReview()` at the moment of a win: after the 5th completed sale, or the first time the income statement shows a positive net income. Apple allows 3 prompts per 365 days per user; it decides whether to show the sheet.
2. Ask the existing Heng Dy Mart-style customers in person this week; ten honest ratings move a listing from "nothing" to "real".
3. Answer every review in App Store Connect; replies show on the page.
